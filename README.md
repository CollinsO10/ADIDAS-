👟 Adidas US Sales Analysis | Excel Dashboard Project

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing Adidas sales performance across the United States between 2020 and 2021 using Microsoft Excel.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights by examining revenue, operating profit, product performance, retailer performance, regional sales distribution, and sales methods.

Through data cleaning, data analysis, Pivot Tables, and interactive dashboard development, this project provides a comprehensive overview of Adidas’ sales performance and identifies opportunities for business growth and improved decision-making.

🎯 Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

* Evaluate Adidas’ overall sales and profitability.
* Identify the highest-performing retailers.
* Analyse sales performance across different US regions.
* Determine the best-performing product categories.
* Compare sales performance across different sales methods.
* Examine monthly and yearly sales trends.
* Evaluate operating profit and profit margins.
* Develop an interactive dashboard for effective business reporting.

📂 Dataset Description

The dataset contains 9,637 sales records covering the period from January 2020 to December 2021.

It includes the following key attributes:

Column	Description
Sales ID	Unique sales record identifier
Retailer	Name of the retailer
Retailer ID	Unique retailer identifier
Invoice Date	Date of the sales transaction
Region	US geographical sales region
State	State where the transaction occurred
City	City where the transaction occurred
Product	Adidas product category
Price per Unit	Selling price per individual unit
Units Sold	Number of units sold
Total Sales	Revenue generated from sales
Operating Profit	Profit generated from sales
Sales Method	Online, Outlet, or In-store
Profit Margin	Percentage of revenue retained as operating profit

💡 Key Analytical Findings

* Adidas generated approximately $120 million in total revenue during the analysis period.
* Total operating profit was approximately $33.16 million.
* The overall operating profit margin was approximately 27.63%.
* The business sold approximately 2.48 million units across 9,637 sales records.
* The dataset covers six retailers, five geographical regions, and six product categories.
* Online, Outlet, and In-store channels provide different routes through which customers purchase Adidas products.
